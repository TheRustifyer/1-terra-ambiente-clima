# Terra, Ambiente e Clima

**Study notes and worked exercises on the Earth’s climate system.**

Physics degree · Faculdade de Ciências da Universidade de Lisboa (FCUL) · First year, first semester · 2026/27

How do the atmosphere, ocean, ice, living organisms, and solid Earth interact? These notes follow that question from observations of present and past climates to the energy exchanges and physical processes that govern them.

The collection follows the course *Terra, Ambiente e Clima*, bringing together explanations, mathematical models, examples, and practice. It is a personal study resource developed throughout the course.
